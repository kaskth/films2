## Why this angle

The obvious films about a crab looking for a shell are the upgrade story, the chase, the nature short and the house hunt. All four were predicted and dropped (`research/predict.md`, `research/overlap.md`). What is left is the arithmetic: a shell is property, property needs defending, and the crowd was never an audience. The film costs one arena, two crabs and seven eyes, and its last line re-reads the first one.
